# about

About me

I’m a student of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science with a strong passion for sustainable technologies, research, and social impact. I enjoy solving technical problems that have real-world implications, especially in areas like environmental engineering, robotics, and community-based innovation.

My work includes research on MHD pumps, wave buoy sensors, and accessible Arduino workshops. I believe in using technology to empower underrepresented communities, simplify complex concepts for learners, and contribute to equitable scientific progress.
